小橘营地隐私政策

小橘营地（Orange Camp）由 Sophia Huang 开发。本政策说明应用如何处理信息。

我们不收集个人数据

小橘营地不要求注册账号，不收集姓名、邮箱、位置、健康数据、设备标识符、使用分析或广告数据，也不会出售或向第三方传输个人数据。

保存在设备上的数据

你的习惯、打卡记录、资源分数和应用状态仅保存在当前设备的应用本地存储中。开发者无法访问这些内容。删除应用通常会同时删除这些本地数据。

第三方服务

当前版本不包含广告、第三方分析、社交登录或云同步服务。应用的 App Store 下载与分发由 Apple 按其自身隐私政策处理。

儿童隐私

由于应用不收集个人数据，因此不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

政策变更

如果未来版本加入同步、分析或其他会改变数据处理方式的功能，本政策将在功能发布前更新。

联系我们

如对本政策有疑问，请联系 sophiahuangjy@gmail.com。

Orange Camp Privacy Policy

Orange Camp is developed by Sophia Huang. This policy explains how the app handles information.

We do not collect personal data

Orange Camp does not require an account and does not collect names, email addresses, location, health data, device identifiers, analytics, or advertising data. We do not sell or transmit personal data to third parties.

Data stored on your device

Your habits, check-ins, resource scores, and app state are stored only in local app storage on your device. The developer cannot access this content. Deleting the app will normally delete this local data.

Third-party services

The current version contains no advertising, third-party analytics, social login, or cloud synchronization. Apple processes App Store downloads and distribution under Apple's own privacy policy.

Children's privacy

Because the app does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If a future version adds synchronization, analytics, or another feature that changes data handling, this policy will be updated before that feature is released.

Contact

Questions may be sent to sophiahuangjy@gmail.com.
